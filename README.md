# aerosight-aircraft-inspection
A computer vision project exploring automated aircraft surface defect detection.
AeroSight — AI-Assisted Aircraft Surface Inspection

Overview

AeroSight is an early-stage engineering project exploring the use of computer vision to identify potential aircraft surface defects in images and organize inspection findings for human review.

The project is being developed as part of the Conrad Challenge 2026–2027 in the Aerospace & Aviation category.

Problem

Aircraft surface inspections require careful examination and documentation. Computer vision may help identify suspicious areas in inspection images and support maintenance teams in reviewing potential defects.

Proposed Approach

AeroSight will investigate whether computer vision models can detect selected surface defects in aircraft images.

The initial prototype will focus on:
	•	Exploring publicly available aircraft surface damage datasets.
	•	Evaluating suitable computer vision methods.
	•	Testing model predictions against labeled images.
	•	Measuring detection performance and documenting limitations.

Development Plan
	1.	Conduct technical research.
	2.	Select and evaluate an appropriate dataset.
	3.	Develop an initial Python prototype.
	4.	Evaluate the model on separate test data.
	5.	Explore a simple interface for demonstrating the results.

Current Status

Stage: Research and planning.

The prototype is not yet complete. Its capabilities and performance will be determined through experiments.

Important Limitations

AeroSight is an educational prototype, not a certified aircraft inspection system. Its predictions will require human review and must not be used as the sole basis for aviation safety or maintenance decisions.
