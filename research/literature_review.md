Literature Review

1. Project Overview

AeroSight is an early-stage engineering project exploring the use of computer vision to identify potential aircraft surface defects in images.

The project is being developed for the Conrad Challenge 2026–2027 in the Aerospace & Aviation category.

2. Research Goal

The goal of this literature review is to understand existing aircraft surface inspection technologies, evaluate current computer vision methods, and identify a potential research gap.

3. Research Questions
	1.	What types of aircraft surface defects can computer vision detect?
	2.	What datasets are available for developing and evaluating detection models?
	3.	What limitations do existing methods have?
	4.	How can model performance and reliability be evaluated?
	5.	What opportunities exist to improve existing approaches?

4. Initial Research Areas
	•	Aircraft surface defect detection
	•	Computer vision and deep learning
	•	Aircraft damage datasets
	•	Model evaluation and reliability
	•	Limitations of automated visual inspection

5. Next Steps
	1.	Review relevant scientific papers.
	2.	Analyze existing commercial inspection solutions.
	3.	Investigate publicly available datasets.
	4.	Identify a specific problem that the project can realistically address.

6. Current Status

The project is currently in the research and planning stage. No prototype has been completed yet.

Research findings and references will be added as the project develops.
